Governance

The [[SHORT FORM]] is overseen by a steering group with representation from all five of the Department’s research communities, reporting to the Department of Physics.

Partners

  • GridPP: through GridPP, Imperial contributes to LondonGrid, one of the UK’s four regional Tier-2 centres supporting the Worldwide LHC Computing Grid.
  • IRIS is a cooperative, community-driven project that develops the digital research infrastructure for STFC-supported science. We host IRIS-funded resources and support IRIS projects.
  • UKRI/STFC funds the national programmes in which we participate.
  • The Research Computing Service provides Imperial’s College-wide high-performance computing, with which we work closely.
