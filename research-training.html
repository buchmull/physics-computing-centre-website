Training

Data-intensive physics needs researchers who combine physical understanding with skills in statistics, machine learning and scientific computing.

The Department offers the MRes in Machine Learning and Big Data in the Physical Sciences, a one-year Master’s course in which students learn to apply machine learning and data-science techniques to real experimental data and carry out an extended research project. [Confirm with the course directors, then keep or delete:] The [[SHORT FORM]] supports project work and teaching connected with the course.

We also run onboarding and practical workshops for researchers and students using our systems, covering software environments, workflows and reproducible computing.
