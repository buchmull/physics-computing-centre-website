What we offer

  • Computing for research

    Batch processing, storage, cloud and GPU resources for projects ranging from exploratory studies to established research programmes, on departmental systems and through national services.

  • Support from physicists and technical staff

    Help choosing the right systems, setting up software and workflows, moving data, and advice on data analysis and machine-learning approaches.

  • A route to larger resources

    Support with access to Imperial’s Research Computing Service and with applications to national infrastructures such as GridPP and IRIS.
